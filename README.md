## Evidence and method

Where a claim matters, I keep a dated receipt. The curated evidence maps for [Flagstone](https://github.com/Skypie99/AccessMap/blob/main/docs/PUBLIC_EVIDENCE.md) and the [portfolio](https://github.com/Skypie99/portfolio/blob/main/docs/PUBLIC_EVIDENCE.md) route to the release records, QA reports and known limitations, so you do not have to dig through hundreds of reports. Release decisions are on the record too, for example the [production approval](https://github.com/Skypie99/AccessMap/pull/35#issuecomment-6394476701) for Flagstone's October web release. Numbers are dated and scoped, past measurements are labeled historical, and known defects stay in the record.
